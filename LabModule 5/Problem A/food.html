About the Festival

Taste of Terengganu brings together the best local food stalls for three days of eating, music and culture by the river. Visitors can try famous dishes such as nasi dagang, keropok lekor and laksam, prepared by home cooks and small businesses from all over the state.

The festival also supports local entrepreneurs and students. Cooking demonstrations are held every evening, and all stalls follow the festival's green guideline by using reusable or biodegradable packaging.

Free entry for all UMT students – show your matric card at the entrance.
